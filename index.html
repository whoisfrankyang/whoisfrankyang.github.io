Bohan Yang

I'm a first-year CS PhD student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Mike Hagenow in the RT² Lab. My current research focuses on human-robot teaming and interactive robot learning. Broadly, I'm interested in making robot policies interpretable and steerable.

Previously, I received my BS in Computer Science from Cornell University, where I was fortunate to work with Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee at the EmPRISE Lab on assistive robotics.
